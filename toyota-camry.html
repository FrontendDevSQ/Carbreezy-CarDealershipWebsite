Description

The 2024 Toyota Camry continues its reputation as a reliable and stylish midsize sedan. Available in various trims, it offers both four-cylinder and V6 engines, with options like the sporty TRD model or the elegant Nightshade edition. The Camry balances comfort, technology, and performance, featuring an 8-speed automatic transmission and hybrid variants for enhanced fuel efficiency. The interior is designed for practicality and elegance, making it suitable for both families and daily commutes.

Car Features

  • 8-speed automatic transmission for the petrol variants
  • Up to 28 MPG city / 39 MPG highway for the 4-cylinder
  • Spacious cabin with comfortable seating for five
  • Optional blind-spot monitoring and rear cross-traffic alert
  • Available premium audio systems

Vehicle History

  • First Generation (1982-1986): The Camry debuted as a compact sedan,
  • Second Generation (1986-1991): This generation introduced a larger body style and additional features
  • Third Generation (1991-1996): The Camry became a midsize sedan during this era, offering improved safety
  • Fourth Generation (1996-2001): This iteration saw a significant design overhaul, with a focus on comfort and handling.
